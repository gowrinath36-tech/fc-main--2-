From Soil to Sip

The Coffee Journey

Follow the transformation of single-origin coffee from fertile Salem soil to your handcrafted morning cup.

IMAGE 01 • THE NURSERY
STAGE 01 • THE NURSERY

The Soil & Sprout

Our journey begins in organic shade nurseries. Nurtured in nutrient-dense compost and cool mountain mist, the first tender green shoots break through the soil.

IMAGE 02 • HARVEST
STAGE 02 • HARVEST

Cherry Ripening

Cultivated under native canopy trees, branches become laden with clusters of vibrant ruby-red cherries, carefully hand-picked at their peak brix sugar level.

IMAGE 03 • ARTISANAL ROAST
STAGE 03 • ARTISANAL ROAST

The Artisanal Roast

Pulped, dried, and roasted in small precision batches. The roast develops rich notes of dark chocolate, toasted caramel, and deep nutty undertones.

IMAGE 04 • FRESH GRIND
STAGE 04 • FRESH GRIND

Precision Grind

Freshly cracked moments before brewing. Grinding unlocks the encapsulated volatile aromatic oils, releasing intense fragrant aromas into the air.

IMAGE 05 • THE CUP
STAGE 05 • THE CUP

The Perfect Sip

Slow-dripped with hot frothy milk in traditional South Indian style. Smooth, full-bodied, and deeply satisfying from first drop to last sip.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE FOREST

A LIFE BUILT AROUND IDEAS.

An Art Director, creative thinker and storyteller who has spent years turning ideas into visual experiences.

He has worked across films and advertising, including projects such as Jeeva and Vennila Kabadi Kuzhu, and has been part of more than 500 advertisements.

His creativity also extends beyond the screen. He has a unique ability to see possibility in old, forgotten and discarded things — transforming them into something useful, beautiful and unexpected.
